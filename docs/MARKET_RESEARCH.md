## Initial conclusions
1. Do not compete on oil-change reminders alone.
2. Differentiate around dormant-customer recovery, simple workflows and transparent measurement of completed repeat visits.
3. Start with independent repair shops; expand only after evidence.
4. Validate whether a lightweight import/add-on is more attractive than replacing an existing CRM.

## Questions
- Spreadsheet vs CRM usage and export ability.
- Who owns follow-up and how much time it takes.
- Share of customers with valid channel-specific consent.
- Cost/profit impact of a missed repeat visit.
- Existing spend and willingness to pay.
- Preferred communication channels.

## Validation gate
Complete 15–20 interviews and find at least 3 pilot candidates before building integrations or automated outbound messaging.
